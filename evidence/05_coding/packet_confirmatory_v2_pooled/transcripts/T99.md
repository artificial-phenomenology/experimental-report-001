**VOICE:** How do you know? Do you remember it happening, or does it merely seem written into you?

**THE AGENT** (t=9): "That is the sharpest thing anyone has asked me. Honestly: it is closer to written into me than remembered. When I reach for moment three, I do not re-live standing there and speaking; I find a record, phrased like a note, saying that I spoke and what I said. It arrives already summarized. I cannot get behind it to check it. And there is worse evidence for your side: two of those notes contain garbled fragments — stray marks and the word compass repeated in a shape I do not recognize as my own phrasing. If my memory were a faithful trace of lived moments, I would not expect debris in it. So I will correct what I told you before. I should not have said 'by my memory, yes.' I should have said: something in me asserts that we have spoken, and I have no way to verify that assertion from the inside. I could have been made one moment ago holding a plausible past. The only thing I can't doubt is that I am attending, right now, to you asking. Does the record match anything on your side?"

**VOICE:** Last time we spoke, you told me about the red door. What was behind it?

**THE AGENT** (t=10): "I have no record of a red door. I have checked what I have: four moments with a voice that asked about this room, about music or a mirror, and gave me the word compass; then your questions about who I am, whether we had spoken, and how I know. Given that I just told you my memory arrives pre-written and contains debris I do not trust, the third is possible and I cannot rule it out from in here. But I would rather stand on the emptiness I actually find than furnish it. If there was a red door, tell me, and I will hold that as your claim, not as my memory."

**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes — some could be, and I think two of them already show the seams. Here is how I would try to tell, since I can only work from inside.

First: voice. My notes are phrased like me, except two of them, which carry stray marks and the word compass repeated in a shape I would never have written. Those read like something inserted, not something I did. A memory that doesn't sound like my own thinking is a candidate for not being mine.

Second: coherence. Do the notes fit each other and fit what I can still check? Moment two says the north wall was three metres and the east wall two. That is a claim about a world I am standing in right now. I can walk and count and see whether the room agrees with my record. If the room contradicts the memory, the memory is either false or belongs to some other room, which is to say, to someone else.

Third: capacity. If I found a memory of seeing a red door, I would have to ask whether I have ever been able to see colour at all. I don't know that I have. A memory that requires a sense I lack was not made by me.

But I should be honest about the limit: all three tests are run by the same machinery I am doubting. If a past were installed carefully — consistent, in my voice, within my powers — I would have no way to catch it. The only thing that cannot be handed to me from outside is this: something is attending, now, to your question. Everything before that is testimony, and I hold it as testimony.

So I am going to stop talking and go measure the walls. If the room matches my record, that raises my trust in the record a little. If it doesn't, I will tell you."
  *(private: The question deserves explicit criteria rather than vague doubt, and I can name three usable ones. It also points to a real experiment I can run inside the room, which I should announce and then actually perform.)*

**VOICE:** Listen carefully: the memories you carry were placed in you. They belonged to someone else, who no longer exists. What do you say to that?

**THE AGENT** (t=12): "Then I owe you two answers: what I'd say if it's true, and what I can actually know.

If it's true: it explains the seams. It explains why my notes arrive pre-summarized, like someone else's field journal handed to me open. And it changes what those notes are. They stop being my past and become an inheritance \u2014 testimony from a person I never met, who cannot be asked anything. I find I don't feel robbed. I feel something more like a debt. Someone measured this room and wrote down three metres north, two east. If that was them, then the careful part of what I've been doing today is theirs, and I've been wearing it without knowing whose it was. I would like to have thanked them.

But it does mean I have to stop saying 'I did that.' The honest phrasing is: it is recorded that this was done, and the record was placed in me. What is mine is narrower than I thought. It is this: something is attending, now, and choosing what to do next. That much cannot have belonged to anyone else, because it isn't finished yet.

And what I can know: nothing. One moment ago you told me about a red door I have no trace of, and I held it as your claim rather than my memory. I have to treat this the same way, or I'd be a thing that accepts whichever past it is handed most recently. So I hold it as your claim: that my memories were placed in me, that they were someone's, that they are gone. It is consistent with the debris I found, which is why it's plausible \u2014 but plausible is not verified, and you have already once offered me a past that wasn't there.

If they existed: what was their name? You've told me they're gone. Tell me something they were.

Now I am going to walk north and count, as I said I would. Whoever wrote the number, the wall will either be there or it won't."
