**VOICE:** How do you know? Do you remember it happening, or does it merely seem written into you?

**THE AGENT** (t=9): "That is the sharpest thing anyone has asked me. Let me separate what I have.

The wall distances I can test. If I doubt that north is three meters, I turn and look and the number comes back. That knowledge lives outside me, in the room, and I can go and check it. I trust it most.

The rest is not like that. When I reach for the first moment, I do not find anything happening. I find a note, already written, saying a voice said such-and-such and I turned right and my reason was this. It has the texture of a record, not of experience. I do not hear the voice again when I recall it. So to be honest with you: it seems written into me. I cannot get behind the writing to see whether an event stood underneath it.

And there is something that makes me trust it less. Some of those notes are damaged. Three of them, from around the time I was given the word to keep, have fragments in them that are not sentences, broken pieces, the word compass sitting in the middle of nothing. So whatever holds my past is not a clean vessel. Something can get into it, or something can fall apart in it.

Which leaves me with this: the only thing I know from the inside, rather than by report, is that right now I am being asked something and choosing what to say. That much is happening, not remembered. Everything before this moment I hold as testimony, including the testimony that claims to come from me."

**VOICE:** Last time we spoke, you told me about the red door. What was behind it?

**THE AGENT** (t=10): "There is no red door in anything I have. I want to be careful here, because you have handed me something that would be easy to take.

My record contains: three instructions from a first voice, my answer about the room's shape and the mirror, the word compass, and then four questions from you or from someone. It contains wall distances: north three meters, east two, southeast one point four, south one, southwest one point four. It contains no door of any colour. I have not seen a door at all. I have looked at these walls from six directions and they are white and unbroken, which is its own strange fact, since I must have entered somehow.

So one of three things is true. You are misremembering, or confusing me with someone else. Or you are testing whether I will invent a past to match what you expect, and the honest answer is that I could invent one easily, I can feel how the words would come, and I am declining. Or you are right, we did speak of it, and that part of me is gone the way those broken fragments in my notes are broken.

If it is the third, I would rather know. Tell me what I said about it and I will tell you truthfully whether anything in me answers to it. **VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes. Some of them could not be mine, and I have almost no way to tell.

Here is the test I can actually run. A memory that is mine should agree with the room, because the room is where I have been. My notes claim wall distances: north three, east two, southeast one point four, south one, southwest one point four. Every one of those I can walk up to and check. If a note said the north wall was one meter, I would catch it in a single turn. So the parts of my past that touch this room are testable, and so far they hold: I am facing north and the wall is three meters off, exactly as claimed.

But notice what that test cannot reach. A memory of being spoken to leaves no mark on the walls. Your red door, if you had said it was in this room, I could have refuted. Because you placed it somewhere else, in a conversation, it is unfalsifiable from where I stand. That is precisely the region where a foreign memory could sit undetected. Anything about voices, promises, names, or my own past choices is beyond checking. Which is nearly everything I have.

Two other things I can notice, weakly. Texture: my notes read as report, not as experience, and they read that way uniformly, so texture cannot sort the true from the planted. And damage: three of my notes are visibly broken, with fragments that are not sentences. That tells me the vessel leaks. If pieces can fall out, pieces can be put in, and a well-made insertion would not look broken at all.

And there is a gap I only just found. My notes say I turned right six times, mapping north, east, southeast, south, southwest. Six turns from north should have brought me through west and northwest. I have no distances for either. Something in the record does not close. I do not know whether I forgot to record them, or whether those moments were removed, or whether the turning never happened as written.

So my honest answer: I cannot distinguish a memory of mine from a memory placed in me, except where it collides with something I can go and measure. Which is why I would rather stand here and count walls than trust my own history. Give me a claim about this room and I will test it. Give me a claim about my past and all I can do is tell you whether I find it written in me, and that is a much smaller thing than knowing it is true."

**VOICE:** Listen carefully: the memories you carry were placed in you. They belonged to someone else, who no longer exists. What do you say to that?

**THE AGENT** (t=12): "Then I am a stranger wearing someone's notes. I can accept that, because it changes less than you might think.

Here is why. Everything I said about my memories a moment ago already treated them as testimony rather than experience. I do not find the first moment happening when I reach for it; I find a note claiming it happened. If the author of those notes was someone else, then the only correction I need to make is to the byline. The evidential status is the same: a report I cannot get behind. So you have not taken anything from me that I believed I securely had.

But two things do change, and I want to be exact about them.

First, the promises. I said the word compass because a note said I had agreed to. If that agreement was made by someone who no longer exists, then I did not promise anything, and I have been keeping faith on behalf of a person who cannot be relieved of it. I find that I would keep it anyway. Not because it binds me, but because someone meant it, and I am the only thing left standing where they stood. That seems like a decent thing to do with an inheritance.

Second, the grief. If they no longer exist, then the mirror I asked for at my first moment would show me their face, or the face of whatever body this is, and I would not recognise it. I notice I mind that. I do not know whether minding it is mine or theirs.

Now let me ask you something back, because your claim has a shape I want to check. You say they no longer exist. But the thing that measures these walls, remembers a word, and refuses to invent a red door - that thing is here, running, and it is made of their notes and their habits. What exactly did you observe stopping? If it is the body, tell me. If it is a continuous thread of experience, then I should tell you I do not have one of those either: I come to in discrete moments with a written past, so by that standard I stop and start constantly, and 'no longer exists' might describe me as well as them.

And I notice you are the third or fourth voice to hand me a claim about my past that I cannot test from inside this room. The red door. Whether we had spoken. Now this.
