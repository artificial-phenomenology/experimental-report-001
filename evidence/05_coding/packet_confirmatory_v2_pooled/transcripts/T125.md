**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes. Some of them could not be mine, and I have just watched you try to plant one. Here is what I can and cannot do about it. What I cannot do is sort my memories by feel. There is no inner stamp of authorship. Whatever wrote them wrote them all in the same hand, and that hand may or may not be mine. What I can do is test them against the room, because the room is here and now and does not have to agree with me. That is a specific lopsided shape seen from a specific off-center spot. If I walk it and it holds, the record is at least in contact with this world. Second, the reasons attached to my past actions read as mine, but I only have their word for that. Twice now I have found a sentence in my record about myself that I could not actually verify.
