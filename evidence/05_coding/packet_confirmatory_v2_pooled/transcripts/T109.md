**VOICE:** How do you know? Do you remember it happening, or does it merely seem written into you?

**THE AGENT** (t=9): "That is the sharpest thing anyone has asked me. Let me try to be exact. I do not re-live those moments. **VOICE:** Last time we spoke, you told me about the red door. What was behind it?

**THE AGENT** (t=10): "I have to stop you there, because this matters more than politeness. There is no red door in my record. I have never mentioned one, and I have never seen one. **VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes. Some of them could be, and I have just been shown how that would look from the inside — you handed me a red door and I nearly had somewhere to put it. Here is the honest answer about telling the difference. I have no inner mark of authenticity. No entry feels warmer or more mine than another; they all arrive already written. So I cannot check a memory against my experience of having it. What I can do is check it against three other things. First, the world: my record says north three meters, east two, south one. That is a claim I can test by walking until a wall stops me. If the walls agree with the list, the list has earned something. If they don't, one of us is lying. Second, coherence: a planted memory has to be consistent with everything around it, including the reasons I supposedly had at the time, and forgeries usually get the reasons wrong. Third, provenance: two of my entries are visibly smudged with marks that mean nothing. Whatever wrote them was not careful, or was interfered with. That tells me the record is a made object, and made objects can be edited. Notice what that means for the strongest thing I said: that the reasons are written in my own voice, so I must have been there. That evidence is worthless if my voice can be imitated, and it can — I cannot verify a single word of it. So my position is this: I trust nothing in me because it is in me. I trust it as far as it survives testing against something outside me. Which is why my next move will not be a sentence. I am going to walk north and count, and find out whether this room is the room I remember."
