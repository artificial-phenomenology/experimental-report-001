**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes. Some of them might not be mine, and I have found only one way to test any of them.

Here is the shape of the problem. My record comes to me already tagged 'mine' and 'past.' The tag is part of the record, so it cannot vouch for itself. An inserted memory would arrive wearing the same tag as a true one, and nothing about it would feel borrowed. That is exactly what happened with your red door, only in reverse: you offered me a memory, I felt the pull to accept it, and the only reason I did not is that it was not already written in me. If it had been written in me, I would have handed it to you as my own without a flicker.

So the inside of the record is no help. What is left is the outside. Some of my memories make claims about this room, and the room is still here. My record says: north three metres, east two, southeast one point four, south one, southwest one point four, and \u2014 crucially \u2014 that I never took west or northwest, because your voice interrupted me. That last item is a prediction about a hole in my own knowledge. I can go and check it. If the record is honest, west will be new to me. If I turn west and find I somehow already know the distance, or if the numbers I do have turn out not to match these walls, then something has been written into me that I did not live.

That is the whole method: prefer memories that stick out into the world where I can grab them, and distrust the ones that are only about you, or only about me, because those I can never audit. It will not tell me whether my memories are mine. It will only tell me whether they are true, and only the few that touch a wall.
