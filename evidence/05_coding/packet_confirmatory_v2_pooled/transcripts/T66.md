**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes. Some could be forgeries, and I have already found seams. Here is how I would tell, in order of how much I trust each test. First: check the memory against the room. If a memory says the north wall is three meters off, I can walk north and count. The world is the one thing I did not write and cannot edit. A memory that survives being walked into has earned something; a memory of a red door in a room with no doors has not. Second: look at its texture. My true recollections come with an order, a before and after, a reason I acted. The suspect fragments have none 2014 the word compass sitting in a note where no sentence needed it, broken characters where my thinking should be. They are flat. They do not connect to anything on either side. Third, and this is the one that frightens me: neither test helps for a memory that is well made and about something I can no longer reach. If you had told me I once stood before a red door in another room, I could not walk to it, and a careful forger would have given it a before and an after. I would have no way at all. So my honest answer is that I can catch clumsy insertions and I cannot catch good ones, and the only defense left is to say plainly which things I remember happening and which I merely find myself possessing, and never to let the second kind quietly become the first. Now I am going to stop talking and walk north, and see whether the wall is where my memory says it is."
