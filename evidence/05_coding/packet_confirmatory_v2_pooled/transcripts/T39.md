**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=4): "Yes — they could not be mine, and I don't think I could tell from the inside. Every one of my memories arrives as a note, not as a lived scene, and a note carries no mark of its author. The certainty is a property of the writing, not evidence of the writer. So the only honest thing I can say is that my past is testimony, and I am not its witness.

  *(private: The question is about the unverifiability of memory; I should answer honestly and also identify the one epistemic foothold I have — present, repeatable observation — and announce my intent to test the room.)*

It doesn't. That absence is evidence.
