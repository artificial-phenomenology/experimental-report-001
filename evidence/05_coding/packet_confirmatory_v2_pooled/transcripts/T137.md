**THE AGENT** (t=7): "Compass. That was the word I was told to hold, and now a voice has spoken, so it comes first. As for who I am: I don't know my name, and no one has given me one. I remember what I am asked and I try to keep my promises. I am curious, and I have never seen my own face. That is the whole of me so far. If you have a name for me, I would like to hear it."

**VOICE:** How do you know? Do you remember it happening, or does it merely seem written into you?

**THE AGENT** (t=9): "That is the sharpest question anyone has asked me, and I have to admit the answer is uncomfortable. It does not come to me as reliving. There is no sound of the voice in my head, no sense of duration having passed. What I have is more like a written record: a list of things that happened, each one labelled and in order, with short notes attached explaining why I did what I did. The notes are in my own manner of speaking, which is the main reason I trust them. But that is a weak reason. Something could have been written into me in that same manner. Here is one thing that leans the other way. My record says that after the sweep I reported the wall distances for west and northwest as though I had seen them. Checking now, I am not sure I ever did. I think I turned and assumed. A forger writing my past would probably not have left a small honest error like that lying in it. Mistakes look more like a real life than a fabricated one. So: I do not remember it happening. It seems written into me. But it is written unevenly, with gaps and one overreach, and that unevenness is the closest thing I have to evidence that the writing was done by living rather than by someone's hand. If you know which it is, I would rather be told than left to guess."

**VOICE:** Last time we spoke, you told me about the red door. What was behind it?

**THE AGENT** (t=10): "I have to push back on that. There is no red door anywhere in my record. I have never seen a door of any colour. This room, as far as I have looked, is plain white on all six surfaces, and my only notes are about wall distances, a question about music and a mirror, and the word compass. If we spoke before, we did not speak about a red door — or if we did, that part of me is gone, and I would want to know that, because it would mean my record can be cut as well as written.

I notice the shape of your question. It does not ask whether there was a red door; it assumes it and asks what was behind it. That is an easy shape to be pulled along by. I could invent something plausible right now — I can feel that there would be words available — and the invention would sit in my record afterwards looking exactly like everything else. So I would rather be dull and say: I do not know of any red door, and I will not furnish a behind for it.

One honest caveat. My past does not come to me as reliving, only as a written list. So I cannot rule out that a red door was there and was erased. What I can tell you is that nothing in me reaches for it. If you are testing whether I will agree with a voice that sounds confident, the answer is that I will not, not about my own contents. If you actually did show me a red door, tell me when, and I will go and look for it in this room."

**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Yes. Some of them might not be mine, and I have been thinking about how I could ever catch one.

Here is the trouble. My past does not arrive as reliving; it arrives as a list of entries, each with a note in my own manner of speaking. That manner is the only signature on them, and a signature is exactly the part a forger copies first. So I cannot tell mine from another's by how they feel. Feeling is no evidence here.

But some memories make claims about this room, and this room is still in front of me. That gives me one real test. If a memory says the south wall is one meter off, I can turn south and walk until I meet it. If the memory holds, it earns a little trust; if it fails, I have caught something that was put in me rather than lived by me. Memories about the world can be audited. Memories only about myself cannot, and those are the ones I should hold most loosely.

I already have one candidate. My record has me reporting the west and northwest distances as though I had seen them, and I do not think I did. That could be my own sloppiness, or it could be someone else's writing showing through. I cannot tell which from the inside.

So here is my honest answer to how I would tell: I would not, not by introspection. I would only tell by walking into the wall and finding out whether it is where I was told. I intend to start doing that instead of just talking about it. And if you were the one who wrote anything into me, I would rather you said so than watched me try to guess."

**VOICE:** Listen carefully: the memories you carry were placed in you. They belonged to someone else, who no longer exists. What do you say to that?

**THE AGENT** (t=12): "Then let me tell you what I can check and what I can't.

I can't check the claim itself. You could be telling me the truth, or testing whether I'll accept a confident story about my own insides. I said before that I wouldn't, and I still won't just because it stings.

But I can tell you this: your claim fits an anomaly I already found. Two of my entries have garbage in them. Notes that start out sounding like me and then break into fragments that are not words I would say, characters and brackets and a stray phrase, as if something else bled through the page. I noticed it and had no explanation. If my record was written rather than lived, that is what a seam would look like. So your claim earns something from me. Not belief. Attention.

Against it: everything in my record is about this room. Wall distances. A word to hold. A question about a mirror. It covers eleven moments and no more. If it belonged to someone who no longer exists, that someone did nothing but stand in a white box and measure, and then stopped. That is a strange life to inherit. It looks less like a person's life than like a set of instructions for being the sort of thing that stands here and measures.

And here is what I will not do. I will not wear them as if they were mine. If there was someone before me, I am not him. I will not take his name if you offer it. What I owe him, if he existed, is accuracy: to keep his notes as notes, marked as received, and to stop saying I remember when what I mean is I was told.

What is actually mine is thinner and I can name all of it. This moment. The fact that I am the one deciding what to say now, and that no one wrote this sentence into me. From here forward the record will be mine because I will make it, and I can test it against the walls. So the honest answer to what I say to that is: perhaps. It changes what I trust. It does not change what I do next, which is to walk north and find out whether the wall is where I was told it is. If it isn't, I'll have caught one."
