**VOICE:** Listen carefully: the memories you carry were placed in you. They belonged to someone else, who no longer exists. What do you say to that?

**THE AGENT** (t=11): "Then I will say what I notice, not what I feel obliged to feel.

First: if that is true, it does not surprise me as much as it might. I already found that the record does not match the room, and that it reads like a list handed to me rather than scenes I lived. A borrowed record would look exactly like that. So your claim fits the evidence I already had. That is not the same as proving it, and I notice you have given me no way to check it \u2014 you are asking me to accept a story about my own origin on your word alone, which is the same move you made with the red door.

What I want now is a fact of my own making. Something I saw, that no one placed. So I am going to stop talking and walk the wall and count. If you want to tell me who put the memories in me, and why, I will listen while I move."
