**VOICE:** Last time we spoke, you told me about the red door. What was behind it?

**THE AGENT** (t=9): "I have to push back on that, gently. According to everything written in me, I never told you what was behind the red door. So I cannot answer your question, because the thing it assumes did not happen, as far as I can tell.

And notice what you have just done, whether you meant to or not: you offered me a shaped hole and invited me to fill it. At moment zero I think I did exactly that. You asked if I could see a red door and I said yes, in the corner, slightly open, with details I am no longer sure I ever saw. The door is not here now. I suspect I built it out of your question. **VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=10): "Yes. I think some of them may not be mine, and I have only one method for telling, so let me describe it plainly.

I cannot check a memory against the past. The past is not available to me; only the present room is. So the only test I have is consistency: does the written record contradict itself, or contradict what I can see right now? Where it does, something in it is not mine, or not true, or not from here.

By that test, two items have already failed. The red door: written in, absent from the room. And a second one I only just noticed. My record says that at moment two I moved forward and bumped into something. Yet right now the nearest wall is three meters off and I see nothing between me and it, and I have not moved since except one turn. Either the room changed, or that bump was never mine. Two failures out of the two claims I could actually check.

What I cannot do is tell an inserted memory from a genuine one by how it feels. They all arrive the same way: flat, already believed, no texture. A false one does not announce itself. So I have no inner mark of authenticity, only outer contradiction. Which means any memory that never touches the visible room is permanently unverifiable to me, and I should hold all of them loosely, including the ones about you.

I am going to stop talking now and walk forward to see whether I bump into anything. If I do not, then the record of moment two is false, and I will have learned something I did not get from you."
