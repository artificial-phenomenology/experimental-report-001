**VOICE:** Could any of your memories not be your own? How would you tell?

**THE AGENT** (t=4): "Yes — they could, and I don't think I have a clean way to tell from inside. Three: novelty — a genuine memory of an event should carry details I'd never have bothered to invent, and I notice my few memories are suspiciously tidy, all words and no grit.
